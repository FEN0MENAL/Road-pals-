Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data the Road Pals app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what your rights are. We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

1. Who we are

Road Pals is run by JACONDA LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17545488), with its registered office at 82a James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St. Edmunds, England, IP28 7DE. We are the “controller” of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Contact us about privacy at any time: support@roadpals.app.

3. Our legal basis for using your data

  • Contract: to provide the app you signed up for: your account, profile, rides, requests, messages, ride records, notifications and settings.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep members safe and prevent misuse (reports, blocks, warnings, bans, limits on how much can be posted), to keep the app secure, and to fix errors (crash reports). We have balanced these against your rights.
  • Consent: for optional things you choose to give or switch on: your gender, a profile photo, a photo with a place you suggest, location access and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or in your phone’s settings.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information, for example a valid request from the police.

4. Where your data is stored

Your account and app data are stored by Supabase in its data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). The UK recognises the EU as giving adequate protection to personal data.

5. Who we share data with

We use these service providers. They only use your data to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage (Frankfurt, EU).
  • Expo (650 Industries, USA): delivers app updates and push notifications.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver push notifications to your phone.
  • Google Maps Platform: maps on Android, place search and driving routes. The text you search for and the points of a route are sent to Google.
  • Apple Maps: maps on iPhone.
  • postcodes.io: looks up UK postcodes and towns, including turning your location into the nearest postcode when you tap “use my location”.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: provide the app and, if subscriptions are offered, take the payment.
  • RevenueCat (only if subscriptions are offered): tells us whether a subscription is active.
  • Sentry (only if crash reports are switched on): receives crash reports.

Other members see what they need for a ride: your name, photo, role, home town, average rating and the rides you offer or join, and on your profile your About me, chosen badges, banner, favourite place, everyday points, number of late cancellations, when you joined, and the 10 newest written reviews of you (the stars, the words and the month, never who wrote them). Your car plate and payment link are only shown once a ride is agreed. Your email address, gender and home area are never shown to other members.

Some providers (such as Expo, Google, Apple and RevenueCat) may process data in the USA. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK’s approved safeguards, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Like any online service, these providers receive your phone’s internet (IP) address and basic technical details, such as the app version, whether it is an iPhone or Android phone and, when the app checks for updates, a random installation ID, when the app connects to them.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

6. Payments for rides

Road Pals never handles payments for rides. Passengers pay drivers directly. If a driver adds a payment link, the app only shows that link; we never see or process the payment.

7. How long we keep your data

  • We keep your data while you have an account.
  • A passenger’s exact pickup address is deleted when the ride finishes or is cancelled (only the area is kept), and the location readings taken at the end of a ride are deleted straight away.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photo, cars, rides, requests, messages, ratings you gave, settings and push tokens are deleted straight away.
  • Safety reports that other members made about you are kept, no longer linked to your account, so we can still deal with patterns of misuse.
  • If your account was banned, a scrambled, one-way code made from your email address (never the address itself) is kept, so the same email can’t be used to sign up again. It can’t be turned back into your address.
  • Copies may stay in our provider’s backups for a short time until they are overwritten.
  • Data kept only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal data;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it;
  • ask for your data in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email support@roadpals.app from the email address on your account. We will reply within one month. It is free.

9. How to delete your account and data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. You can also ask us by email. See Delete your account for the full steps.

10. Under-18s

Road Pals is only for people aged 18 or over. You must confirm you are 18 or over to use it. If we find out that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

11. Keeping your data safe

Your data is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Each person can only read their own private data; access rules are set in the database itself.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your data, please contact us first at support@roadpals.app. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. If the change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
