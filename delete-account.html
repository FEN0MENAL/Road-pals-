Delete your Road Pals account

You can delete your Road Pals account and its data at any time. It is free.

In the app

  1. Open Road Pals and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email privacy@CHANGE-ME.example from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Road Pals account”. We will check the request is from you, delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and profile (name, photo, home area, gender and preferences)
  • Your cars, payment link, rides, requests and bookings
  • Your messages, ride records and the ratings you gave
  • Your settings and push notification tokens

What may be kept

  • Safety reports that other members made about you are kept, no longer linked to your account, so we can still deal with misuse.
  • Copies may stay in our provider’s backups for a short time until they are overwritten.
  • Anything we must keep by law.

If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
